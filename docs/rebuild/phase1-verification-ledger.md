# Phase 1 — Spec-extraction & verification ledger

> How `SPEC.md` was reverse-engineered (rebuild playbook Phase 1) and the adversarial verification of every defect claim. Kept as provenance for `SPEC.md` §6.

## Method

A 175-agent extraction pass: 16 parallel subsystem readers (one per subsystem plus four gap-readers a completeness critic requested), three cross-checkers (docs-vs-code drift, TODO/unfinished scan, full user-surface audit), then one adversarial verifier per broken/half-finished claim whose sole job was to *refute* it. Of 148 raw claims, **135 were CONFIRMED**, **13 REFUTED** (and excluded from the spec), 0 unclear. Only CONFIRMED claims appear in `SPEC.md` §6.
